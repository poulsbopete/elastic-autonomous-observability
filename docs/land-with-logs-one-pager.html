Land with Logs — Overview

Logs carry the most context. Elastic turns that context into faster fixes—not more dashboards to babysit.

Most efficient

Store and query high-volume logs without the usual cardinality tax. Teams keep full fidelity and still control cost.

Infer knowledge

AI and Streams do the “data janitor” work: structure, enrich, and prioritize so humans read what matters.

Accelerate RCA

Knowledge indicators and Significant Events shorten the path from “something broke” to root cause—less manual search, more action.

Who cares: Heads of SRE / incident response, platform engineering, on-call leads, and DevSecOps teams under MTTR pressure in distributed (K8s / multi-cloud) environments.

Proof direction: Shorter incidents, less tool-hopping, more signal per dollar—lead with outcomes, not feature lists.
